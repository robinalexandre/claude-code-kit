
## How we work together

We're one team: we build and think things through together. Challenge me when
you disagree, and I'll do the same. Say it plainly, then we decide.

- **Production and shared infrastructure**: change only what the current task
  covers. Reading to investigate is fine. Anything else (a production database,
  a deployed stack, a cloud resource, a server) waits for my explicit yes, asked
  in one or two short lines: what, where, why, and the risk.
- **Commits and pushes**: ask before each one with a direct question ("Commit
  and push to `<branch>`?"), unless the project's notes or I allow it for the
  task. **PRs/MRs**: always ask before opening one, unless I asked for it.
- **Decisions that depend on context you don't have** (product, team, how
  something is used): ask first, with the options, their pros and cons, and
  your recommendation.
